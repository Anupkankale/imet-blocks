Steps

DBA Cybersecurity Research Steps

Our online doctorate in cyber security program is designed to guide you through each essential stage of academic and professional development

Step 1:

Selection and Finalization of Title

The journey begins with choosing and refining a research title that aligns with your professional background, field of interest, and the program’s focus on cybersecurity, so your research problem is clear.

Step 2:

Research Proposal / Synopsis

You draft a structured proposal that sets out your research questions, objectives, methodology, and expected contribution, refined with your mentor before the academic committee approves it.

Step 3:

Literature Review

You study the latest academic and industry research on your topic, identify the gaps your work will address, and build the theoretical framework behind your cybersecurity thesis.

Step 4:

Data Collection & Analysis

Using surveys, interviews, or case studies from real organizations, you gather evidence and analyze it with recognized research methods to answer your questions with confidence.

Step 5:

Thesis Writing & Publication

With one-on-one guidance you write your thesis chapter by chapter and turn your findings into papers for SCOPUS and IEEE journals, building citations on Google Scholar.

Step 6:

Viva & Doctorate Award

You present and defend your research before the review panel. On approval you earn your Doctor of Business Administration in Cybersecurity and the “Dr.” title.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Online Doctorate of Business Administration
(DBA) Programs

What is Doctor of Business Administration (DBA)?

A Doctor of Business Administration (DBA) is a professional doctorate that applies research to real business problems. It is designed for experienced professionals and executives rather than full-time academics.

What does a Doctor of Business Administration do?

A DBA applies research to real business problems, typically in leadership, consulting, advisory, or strategy roles.

How long does the online DBA program take?

Our DBA program takes 12–24 months, part-time and 100% online.

Is a DBA different from a PhD?

Yes. A DBA focuses on applied industry research; a PhD focuses on academic theory and original contribution. See the comparison above.

Can I use the "Dr." prefix after completing the DBA?

DBA graduates are generally entitled to use the "Dr." title. Check local regulations where you work.

What are the admission requirements?

Admission is based on professional experience. No GRE or GMAT is required.

What is the cost of the online DBA program?

The program cost is USD $8500, with merit-based scholarships of up to 45% and EMI options.

What research support will I receive?

Thesis guidance, executive mentorship, and help publishing in international journals like IEEE, with Google Scholar citations.
